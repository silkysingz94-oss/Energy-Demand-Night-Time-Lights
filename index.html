Methodology

Household electricity demand is calculated by multiplying the selected household attribute by the assumed daily household demand. Commercial and public-service demand are additional percentages of household demand. Annual demand is daily demand multiplied by 365. Forecasts use compound annual growth.

Population density is calculated from population divided by the GeoJSON Area_sqkm attribute. Missing or zero area values are excluded. Estimates are scenarios, not measured electricity consumption.
