Methodology

Population and household totals are aggregated from the selected enumeration areas.

Household demand = total households × assumed daily electricity demand per household.

Commercial demand = household demand × commercial demand percentage.

Public services demand = household demand × public services demand percentage.

Annual demand = total daily demand × 365.

Forecast = baseline annual demand × (1 + annual growth rate)n.
