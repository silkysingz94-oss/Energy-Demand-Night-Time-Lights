METHODOLOGY & INTERPRETATION
1. Geographic data
Enumeration-area polygons and their population and household attributes are read directly from the GitHub GeoJSON.

2. Household demand
Household count is multiplied by the assumed daily electricity demand per household.

3. Additional demand
Commercial and public-services demand are added as percentages of household demand.

4. Forecast
Annual demand is projected using the selected annual growth assumption and forecast horizon.

5. Interpretation
Results are indicative planning scenarios. They do not represent metered consumption, utility supply, grid capacity or an official energy forecast.
