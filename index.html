Methodology

Household demand is estimated as the number of households multiplied by the assumed daily household electricity demand. Commercial and public-service demand are added as percentages of household demand. Annual demand is daily demand multiplied by 365. Forecast demand uses compound annual growth.

These are scenario estimates, not measured electricity consumption or utility-meter observations. Results depend on the selected assumptions and source attributes.
